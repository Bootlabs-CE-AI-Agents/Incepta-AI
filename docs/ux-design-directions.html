Make Your Decision

Based on your goal of "Modern & Innovative" for an enterprise B2B AI platform:

Direction 1: Evolved Liquid Glass

Best if you want maximum visual impact and your users appreciate "premium" aesthetics.

Risk: May feel dated by late 2025

Direction 2: Modern Minimal

Best for maximum professionalism and if your users prioritize efficiency over aesthetics.

Risk: May feel "generic"

Direction 3: Hybrid + Fluid Animation (Recommended)

Professional base + subtle animated orbs that reveal through glass panes for futuristic AI feel.
